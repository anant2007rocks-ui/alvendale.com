M&A Advisory

From first outreach to
closing the deal.

Boutique M&A advisory for healthcare and general industry operators. We source targets, run acquisition outreach, and prepare business owners for a transaction — building their CIM and positioning them to negotiate from strength.

What We Do

Two sides of every deal.

Deal Sourcing & Acquisition Outreach

We source targets for PE operators and rollup platforms in healthcare and general industry. Outreach is direct and relationship-driven, focused on off-market opportunities not yet shopped to brokers.

Target identification based on buyer criteria
Cold and warm outreach to business owners
Initial screening and qualification
Off-market deal flow generation

CIM Preparation & Seller Advisory

We work with owners considering a sale — building the CIM, clarifying valuation, and preparing them to negotiate with buyers from an informed position.

CIM drafting and financial narrative
Business valuation context and benchmarking
Negotiation preparation and deal structure
Financial clean-up and presentation

Sector Focus: Healthcare Services

Active coverage of dental, veterinary, behavioral health, med spa, physical therapy, and home health — sectors where our scientific and regulatory knowledge creates real sourcing edge.

Dental & DSO rollup support
Behavioral health & mental health platforms
Med spa, aesthetics, and wellness
Home health & care management

General Industry Advisory

Deal sourcing for blue-collar service rollups — HVAC, landscaping, plumbing, and cleaning — where PE consolidation is active and our process applies directly.

Blue-collar service business sourcing
Owner outreach and relationship building
Business readiness assessment
Transaction preparation support
Our Process

How we work.

01
Define Criteria

Define target profile: sector, size, geography, margin, and deal structure.

02
Source & Outreach

Direct cold and warm outreach. Off-market focus to avoid broker competition and premium.

03
Qualify & Prepare

Screen against criteria. Build the CIM and financial narrative before any buyer conversation.

04
Support to Close

Advise through LOI, diligence, and negotiation. Help sellers defend valuation and understand deal structure.
